Quality & Handling

Quality is a process, not a tagline.

Our quality approach brings together inspection, grading, careful handling and dispatch discipline across the supply flow.

Quality framework

From arrival to dispatch.

Each stage is designed to protect appearance, condition and consistency from arrival through dispatch.

01

Incoming inspection

Check visible condition, maturity, damage, packaging and arrival quality.

02

Grading & sorting

Separate produce by size, appearance and customer requirement before dispatch.

03

Hygienic handling

Keep handling areas, packing materials and movement practices suitable for fresh produce.

04

Cold-chain awareness

Use appropriate temperature-controlled storage or movement where the fruit requires it.

05

Pack & dispatch

Prepare orders around quantity, pack format and delivery requirements.

06

Customer feedback

Use buyer feedback to improve selection, grading and service consistency.
