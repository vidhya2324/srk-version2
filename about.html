About SRK Fruits

A fruit business built on market understanding, relationships and consistency.

SRK Fruits brings together international sourcing, domestic fruit supply and wholesale distribution from the heart of Chennai's Koyambedu market.

Careful selection · dependable supply

Our story

From knowing the market to building a wider supply network.

SRK Fruits grew from a close understanding of Chennai's fruit trade where timing, quality, relationships and reliability matter every day. The business has carried that practical market experience into a more organised sourcing and distribution model.

Under the leadership of Mr. Sivaramakrishnan, Managing Director, the company continues to develop its sourcing network while staying close to the realities of retailers, wholesalers and other trade buyers.

Today, SRK Fruits operates as an importer and wholesale distributor, bringing selected imported varieties together with domestic and seasonal fruits for customers across India.

“The goal is simple: source with care, handle with discipline and build relationships that last beyond a single order.”

Storage & distribution

Handling that respects the fruit after it arrives.

Imported fruit needs more than a good source. Storage, movement, timing and dispatch all influence the condition in which it reaches the buyer.

Our operating approach is built around appropriate handling, storage and distribution practices for the product and its requirements, with temperature-controlled practices used where the fruit calls for them.

  • Arrival handling
    Condition is considered before fruit moves into the next stage.
  • Storage discipline
    Products are handled according to their storage and movement needs.
  • Planned dispatch
    Orders are coordinated around quantity, destination and timing.
